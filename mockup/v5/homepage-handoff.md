# Homepage handoff · Vietnamese landing page

[Open the homepage review](website-review.html). The current Primitive AI site supplies the first and last sections. The Vietnamese customer presentation informs the product story in the middle. The [Vietnamese slide reference](slides-vi.html) keeps all original compositions available for comparison. The six application examples and measured runs share a carousel on the [Products preview](products.html).

The first problem chapter shows the time spent finding and re-entering information. The second shows how work stalls between tools even after the data is available. The product answers both problems by retrieving authorized information and carrying out approved steps across existing tools. The value cards define the same two measures the rollout uses to compare ways of working. Integration explains where the agents work, and the controls chapter explains what people can approve or review. Customer and partner experience leads into the costs for the selected scope and the invitation to discuss a workflow.
