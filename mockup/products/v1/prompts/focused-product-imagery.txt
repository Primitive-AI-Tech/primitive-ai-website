# VisionHub
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: landscape product-page card artwork for a B2B AI products website
Primary request: create a refined editorial image suggesting workplace computer vision in industrial operations.
Scene/backdrop: a clean, modern manufacturing line viewed at a shallow elevated angle, with a precision camera inspecting a component as it moves through the line.
Subject: one real industrial production moment, orderly machinery and a single item under inspection.
Style/medium: premium editorial product photography with subtle photographic illustration polish, tactile detail, realistic materials, composed for a calm enterprise website.
Composition/framing: wide 3:2 composition; clear focal point near center; enough quieter area toward the upper left for an overlaid small label outside the image in the webpage.
Lighting/mood: crisp daylight mixed with soft factory light, capable and calm.
Color palette: neutral steel and warm industrial tones with restrained sapphire-blue light accents inspired by #044eff.
Materials/textures: brushed metal, glass optics, clean conveyor surfaces.
Constraints: no brand names, no logos, no readable text, no dashboards, no fake UI, no charts, no watermark. This is conceptual artwork, not evidence of a specific deployed installation.
Avoid: sci-fi factory, neon overload, clutter, dramatic danger, human faces, extra cameras, malformed machinery.
